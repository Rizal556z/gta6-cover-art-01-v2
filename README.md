Vice City Cover Studio turns your photos and a caption into GTA 6-style multi-panel cover art — 6 layouts, 6 Vice City colour grades, per-panel crop and zoom, caption, subtitle and badge text, grain, vignette and halftone, and one-click PNG export in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080. Runs fully offline in the browser; photos never leave your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
